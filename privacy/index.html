Privacy policy

Who I am

This website belongs to Aleksander Goliunov, a physical performance coach for racing drivers, registered as an individual entrepreneur in Georgia. Contact: aleksandrgoliunov@yahoo.com.

What I collect

  • Audit and contact forms: your name, email, WhatsApp number, Instagram, series, team, what you tell me about your body in the car, and any video links you choose to send.
  • Booking a call: the name, email and time you enter in the Google Calendar booking page.
  • Reaction test: the name you type and your reaction times, shown on the public leaderboard.

The site has no advertising trackers and no analytics.

Why

Only to prepare your audit, reply to you, run our calls and, if we work together, your programme. I don't sell or share your details for marketing.

Where it is stored

In my private Google account (Google Sheets and Google Calendar) and, for messages from the contact form, in my email via Formspree. These services may process data outside your country under their own safeguards.

How long

Until you ask me to delete it, and no longer than two years after our last contact. Health-related details you share are kept to the minimum needed for your programme.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details at any time — email me and I'll do it within 30 days. To remove a reaction test result, email me the name you used.
